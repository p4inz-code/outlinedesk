How it works

Say what waits for what. The plan does the rest.

Try it below. This demo runs the same scheduling engine as the app, in your browser, and sends nothing anywhere.

Live demo

A five task shot

Change a length with the plus and minus buttons. Work days are Monday to Friday and the shot starts Monday 9 November 2026. Comp must finish by Friday 20 November.

The three steps

What you do in the app

  1. Add the work. Make a card for each asset, sequence, shot and task. Give it a length in working days and an owner. Import a sheet if you have one.
  2. Link what waits for what. Connect Animation to Layout and it knows Animation cannot start first. Comp can wait for both Lighting and Sound.
  3. Read the plan. Dates, the critical path, progress, risks and workload are calculated. Mark work done and the plan updates.

When something slips

What happens next

1

Every later date moves

You change one length. Every card that waits for it, directly or through others, gets new dates.

2

Late work is flagged

A card due before its calculated finish is marked late, and the Home page lists it with the reason.

3

You see the cost

The critical path and the forecast show which delay moves the finish date and which has room to slip.
