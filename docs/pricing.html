How to buy

  1. Email atharva.patil.cg@gmail.com with the plan you want.
  2. You get UPI payment details.
  3. After the payment arrives, your licence key and receipt are emailed, usually within one business day.
  4. In Outlinedesk, open Settings › Licence, paste the key and choose Activate.

Receipts are payment receipts, not GST tax invoices: P4inz Interactive Labs is not GST-registered yet. Studios that require a GST invoice cannot buy until that changes. Prices outside India are not available yet; write to the address above.
