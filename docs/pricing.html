What every plan includes

  • A free 27-day trial of everything before you pay.
  • All updates for the term. After a plan ends, projects stay readable and exportable (a 7-day grace, 3 days on monthly plans).
  • E-mail support is free for your first year; after that it is an optional add-on (₹99 Solo, ₹499 Team, ₹999 Studio, ₹1,999 Large a month). Priority support (4-hour reply) is ₹999 a month on top.
  • 14-day refund on your first payment, no questions asked.
  • Founder price lock: if you start within the first 12 months after launch, your plan price stays the same for 24 months.

How to buy

  1. Email atharva.patil.cg@gmail.com with the plan you want.
  2. You get UPI payment details.
  3. After the payment arrives, your licence key and receipt are emailed, usually within one business day.
  4. In Outlinedesk, open Settings › Licence, paste the key and choose Activate.

Receipts are payment receipts, not GST tax invoices: P4inz Interactive Labs is not GST-registered yet. Studios that require a GST invoice cannot buy until that changes. Prices outside India are not available yet; write to the address above.
