How to buy

Four steps

  1. Email atharva.patil.cg@gmail.com with the plan you want.
  2. You get UPI payment details.
  3. After the payment arrives, your licence key and receipt are emailed, usually within one business day.
  4. In Outlinedesk, open Settings, then Licence, paste the key and choose Activate.

Receipts are payment receipts, not GST tax invoices: P4inz Interactive Labs is not GST-registered yet. Studios that require a GST invoice cannot buy until that changes. Prices outside India are not available yet.

Questions

About buying

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every copy starts with 27 days of everything, and you do not need to register.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of your first payment, no questions asked.

Do you offer a student price?

Yes, ₹299 a year for one verified student at the launch price, and ₹649 a year after the launch year.

Can I change plan later?

Yes. Moving up costs the difference for the rest of the period. A smaller plan applies from the next period.

Do you accept cards or international payments?

Not yet. Payment is by UPI for now. Write to us if you are outside India.
