Privacy

Your project data never leaves your computer.

Projects
Stored in a file on your disk. Outlinedesk does not upload them anywhere.
Licence keys
Checked on your computer with a built-in public key. No account, no sign-in.
Telemetry
None. Outlinedesk does not report usage.
AI agents
The optional agent connection (MCP) uses a local pipe or socket on your computer, not the network.
This website
No cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts or fonts.
When you email me
I keep your name, email, plan and payment reference to issue your key, send receipts and remind you before renewal. I do not sell or share them.
