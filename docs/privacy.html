Privacy

Your project data never leaves your computer

No account, no upload, no usage reports, no cookies. Here is the full list of what Outlinedesk does with the network, and a switch to see it with the update check on or off.

What leaves your computer?

With the update check on

ItemWhat happens
Your projectsStay in files on your disk or your shared folder. Never uploaded.
Your licence keyChecked on your computer with a built-in public key. Never sent anywhere.
Usage and crashesNot reported. Crash details stay in a local file that you can copy yourself.
Update checkThe app asks GitHub for one small file that lists the newest version. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any download. No project data, licence key or identifier is sent.
The optional AI connectionUses a local pipe on your computer. It does not use the network.

With the update check on, the only thing that can leave your computer is a request for the update file.

This website

No cookies, no trackers

  • No cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • The few scripts on this site are small local files for the calculator, demo and checkers. They send nothing and store nothing.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs.

When you email us

What we keep

If you buy a plan, we keep your name, email address, plan and payment reference. We use them to issue your key, send receipts and remind you before renewal. We do not sell or share them. A licence key holds a one-way hash of your email address, never the address itself.
