About

Made by someone who plans productions

Outlinedesk is made by Atharva Patil at P4inz Interactive Labs in Navi Mumbai, India. It exists because small studios plan in spreadsheets that go stale, and the hosted trackers that fix that cost more and ask for more than a small team wants to give.

Principle 1

Your data stays yours

Your project is a file in your own storage. The app works offline and sends nothing anywhere except an optional update check.

Principle 2

Say what it does not do

No asset storage, no review video, no live editing. We would rather tell you now than after you buy.

Principle 3

Fair licensing

A free 27-day trial, a refund in 14 days, and when a plan ends the app goes read-only. Your work is never held hostage.
