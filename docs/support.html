Everyone else

Common questions

Where do I enter my licence key?

Settings (Ctrl+,), then Licence. Paste the whole key, including the DESK- start, and choose Activate.

My plan ended. Did I lose my work?

No. Outlinedesk becomes read-only: open, view and export everything. Enter a renewal key to edit again.

Windows says the publisher is unknown.

The installers are not code-signed yet. In the SmartScreen window choose More info, then Run anyway.

Can several people edit one plan at the same time?

Not live. A project is one file; teams share it on a drive, and Outlinedesk merges changes safely when two people save.

Outlinedesk didn't close properly. Where is my work?

Open Outlinedesk again. It offers the latest autosave, which is never more than two minutes old.
