Where Outlinedesk fits, and where it does not

Studios plan in a spreadsheet, in a hosted production tracker, or in a general task tool. Here is an honest look at each.

Next to hosted production trackers

Tools such as Flow Production Tracking (ShotGrid), ftrack and Kitsu run on a server, usually in the cloud, and charge per person per month. Check each vendor's own pricing page for current prices.

When to choose something else

Choose a hosted tracker if you need live editing for many people in a browser, built-in review and playback, or deep integrations with your tools today.

Choose Outlinedesk if you want planning and tracking without a server, if client rules keep your data off the cloud, if the per-seat price is the obstacle, or if you want a planner beside the tool you already use.

Can I use Outlinedesk beside a hosted tracker?

Yes. Many teams plan departments in Outlinedesk and keep review and delivery in another tool. You can import a tracker's export as a CSV file.

Can I move from a tracker to Outlinedesk?

Import the CSV export and map the columns. Presets for common trackers are included.
