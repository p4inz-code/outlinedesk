# Frequently asked questions

Does it need internet? No. The app works offline. The only network use is the optional update check, which you can turn off in Settings. A licence key is checked on your own computer, with no internet.

Where is my data? In your project file or team folder on your storage. We never receive it.

What happens when my plan ends? There is a short grace period, then the app becomes read-only: projects still open and export. A new key unlocks editing.

Can several people work on one project? Yes, with a team folder on shared storage. See TEAM_FOLDERS.md.

Does it replace Excel? It reads and writes Excel files, adds your own columns and formulas, and adds what sheets cannot do: schedule engine, dependencies, history, merging, reports.

Does it store assets? No. It links to files and can flag when they change.

What does it cost? See the pricing page. Monthly or yearly, student price, 27-day trial, 14-day refund.

Is support included? E-mail support is free for your first year, then an optional add-on.

Can we deploy it to many computers? Yes. Ask by email for the studio kit, which has a policy file, an offline installer bundle and an IT guide.
