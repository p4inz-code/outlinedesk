# How licences work

## Trial

Every copy starts with a 27-day trial of all features. You do not need to register or give an email address. The trial days left appear at the bottom left of the window and on Settings, Licence.

## Buying and activating

1. Email atharva.patil.cg@gmail.com with the plan you want.
2. You receive UPI payment details. After the payment arrives, a licence key and a receipt come back by email, usually within one business day.
3. In Outlinedesk open Settings, then Licence. Paste the whole key (it starts with DESK-) and press Activate. You can also drop the `licence.deskkey` file on that page.

The key is checked on your computer. It does not need the internet. Receipts are payment receipts. They are not GST tax invoices, because P4inz Interactive Labs is not GST-registered yet.

## What the key contains

The key holds the plan, the number of seats, the start and end dates and the name it was issued to. It holds a one-way hash of your email address, never the address itself.

## Seats

Seat limits are an agreement, not a technical lock. The app shows how many seats your plan includes. Each person who edits a project should have one.

## When a plan ends

A plan has a short grace period after its end date: 7 days for yearly plans and 3 days for monthly plans. After that, Outlinedesk becomes read-only. Every project still opens and exports, so your work is never held hostage. A new key from a renewal unlocks editing again. Renew by replying to the licence email before the end date.

## Renewals, refunds and changes

- Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. The key ends at the end of the paid month.
- The first payment has a 14-day refund, no questions asked. After that, refunds are given only for a defect that cannot be fixed.
- Moving to a bigger plan costs the difference for the rest of the period. A smaller plan applies from the next period.
- A lost key is sent again for free.

## Price lock and support

Prices are fixed for the first 12 months after launch. A customer who starts in that first year keeps the same price for 24 months from their start date. Email support is free for the first 12 months of each customer, then it is an optional monthly add-on.

## Student plan

The student plan is for one verified student. Verification is done once by hand, with a college email address or a student ID. The launch price is 299 rupees a year, limited to the first 500 student keys. After the launch year the price is 649 a year. Students who joined at the launch price keep it while they are enrolled, for up to three years.

## Revoked keys

If a key is refunded or misused, it is added to a signed revocation list. The list reaches the app through the update check. If you turn the update check off, the app cannot learn about revoked keys.

## Studios with many computers

A studio can put one key in a policy file for every computer, so nobody has to paste it. Ask by email for the deployment kit.
